# VISION.md — Dev-angPatil Profile Repository

## Vision
The `Dev-angPatil` profile repository represents the primary technical gateway and public identity for Devang Patil on GitHub. Its vision is to project the caliber, precision, and architectural discipline of an elite systems engineer and AI researcher.

## Target Audience
- Senior Systems Engineers & Technical Leads
- Founders & Research Scientists
- Open-Source Collaborators & Engineering Hiring Managers

## Core Problem Solved
Most developer profiles suffer from severe signal degradation: animated badges, skill-icon walls, mini-games, and vanity statistics that distract from genuine engineering depth. This profile replaces low-signal noise with high-density technical substance.

## Success Criteria
1. **Immediate Clarity:** Any technical visitor immediately understands Devang's domain focus within 5 seconds.
2. **Definitive Flagship:** Spotlights a single flagship system (`ops-assistant`) with architectural substance, benchmark data, and real engineering rigor rather than superficial lists.
3. **Taste & Restraint:** Zero gimmicks, zero clutter, elegant typography, resilient across dark and light themes.
